# ORCA assembly illustrations

Source: ORCA project, Soft Robotics Lab, ETH Zurich.

The 154 illustrations accompanying 29 published steps are copied unchanged.
`diagrams.json` records the original URL for each illustration. Local filenames
have been shortened for serving. No image colours, annotations, or content have
been changed.

Original image captions are included beneath their respective illustrations.
Markdown formatting is removed for plain-text display. Image-only source views
receive a generic reference-view label. The condensed checklist remains separate
and follows its explicitly associated image group.

The website's checklist text is a condensed, reorganised, plain-language
adaptation of the corresponding official step pages, with source links on each
step. It is separate from the original illustrations. Steps 04, 27, and 28 have
no standalone pages in the published directory as inspected on 2026-09-10.

Adaptation and interface: Orca Atlas. This independent educational companion is
not endorsed by ETH Zurich or the original ORCA project.
